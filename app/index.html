About this tool

Purpose. Stress and fatigue degrade attention, decision-making and communication in the cockpit. This tool gives pilots a quick, structured way to notice when they are drifting toward an unsafe state, and to see patterns over time.

What it is built on. The FAA IM SAFE personal checklist, the Samn-Perelli fatigue scale used in aviation fatigue research, and the Perceived Stress Scale (Cohen, Kamarck & Mermelstein, 1983). The scoring rules are simple and transparent. See the research documents in the repository for the evidence and the reasoning.

What it is not. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic instrument, and not a substitute for an aviation medical examiner, a fatigue risk management system, or professional mental-health care. A Green result does not certify fitness; a Red result is an invitation to stop and think, and to talk to someone.

Privacy. All data is stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export if you want to keep a copy, and Delete all data if you want it gone.

If you need help now. If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately. Many airlines and pilot associations run confidential peer support programmes. Reaching out is a sign of good judgement, not weakness.
